# B1 — The Glacier cost gotcha

What's the actual cost impact of an early deletion after archival? How would you redesign the
lifecycle so this stops quietly costing more than budgeted?

Answer:

The photo moves to Glacier after 30 days. If it is deleted on the day 40. So it will be 10 days only after moving to Glacier. The minimum retention policy for Glacier is 90 days. Then we will be paying for the next 80 days also even though the photo is deleted. 

To redesign this, we can hold early deletion in standard s3 itself and move others only Glacier.
